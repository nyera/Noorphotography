IMPORTANT: This theme package does NOT bundle the wedding/portrait/fashion/

with no verifiable commercial redistribution license, and are therefore
excluded from this distributable package (see /documentation/licensing.md
for the full audit).

Before deploying or reselling a site built on this theme:
  - Replace all portfolio/service/testimonial/hero images with your own
    photography, or with stock images you hold a commercial license for
    (e.g. a paid Unsplash+/Adobe Stock/Shutterstock license, or an
    equivalent extended license covering resale-adjacent use).
  - The theme's demo importer (Theme Settings → محتوى تجريبي) works
    without images and will create all demo content (projects, services,
    testimonials, packages, pages) with no featured image set; you attach
    your own licensed images afterwards from the Media Library.
